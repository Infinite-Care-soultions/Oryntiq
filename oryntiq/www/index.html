Why Business Central

An end-to-end view of your business

Dynamics 365 Business Central helps manufacturers and growing companies respond quickly to changing market and production demands — connecting sales, finance, supply chain, and service in one adaptable ERP.

Manage financial data

Accelerate financial close and reporting with accuracy, while ensuring compliance.

  • Connect accounting, sales, purchasing, inventory, and customer data
  • Chart performance in real time with built-in Power BI reports
  • Refine forecasts across multiple dimensions with Excel integration

Automate the supply chain

Increase visibility and gain greater control over purchasing and inventory.

  • Predict replenishment with dynamically updated stock levels
  • Reduce shortages with lead times, reorder points, and substitutes
  • Protect profitability with vendor discounts and approval workflows

Sell smarter & serve better

Maximize revenue and improve customer service with built-in recommendations.

  • Prioritize leads and surface up-sell, cross-sell, and renewal opportunities
  • Handle inquiries, service, and payments from within Outlook
  • Assign the right skills and accelerate case resolution
